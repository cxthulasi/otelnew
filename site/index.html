The platform

Traditional monitoring wasn't built for this.

Modern systems are distributed, dynamic, and noisy. Traditional monitoring tools weren't built for them.

otelnew is a next-generation observability platform designed from the ground up around OpenTelemetry, the open standard for telemetry data. It correlates logs, metrics, and traces automatically, so engineers can follow a request across services, spot anomalies in real time, and pinpoint root causes without switching between tools.

Because it's built on open standards, your instrumentation stays portable and your data stays yours. Whether you're running Kubernetes, serverless, or a hybrid cloud, otelnew gives you full-stack visibility without the complexity or the lock-in.

  • Instrument once One open standard for logs, metrics, and traces, across every service.
  • See the whole request Follow a call across services without reconstructing the path by hand.
  • Keep the data Portable instrumentation, and telemetry that isn't locked to one vendor.

How it works

One pipeline from instrumentation to answer.

  1. Instrument once

    Emit logs, metrics, and traces with OpenTelemetry. The same standard covers every service you run.

  2. Correlate automatically

    otelnew stitches those signals together, so a spike, a span, and a log line land on the same request.

  3. Resolve in minutes

    Go from the alert to the failing service and the line that explains it, without opening another product.

Where it runs
